How to blur a picture on iPhone—including shots you did not take

Last updated: September 2026

How to blur a picture on iPhone includes more than photos you captured. Recents holds screenshots, WhatsApp saves, cropped exports. Those files have no Portrait depth. You still may want the main object sharp and the rest quiet.

A picture on iPhone is any still image in Photos. How to Blur Photo opens those files, detects a subject when one exists, and blurs the background or applies a full-image style without a Mac.

Pictures without depth data

Screenshot of a story, a downloaded JPEG, a frame grabbed from video—none of these have Portrait metadata. Built-in depth editors will not help. An after-the-fact blur app is the practical path.

If the picture has no clear subject (a texture, a crowd), use a lighter full-frame gaussian instead of forcing a fake portrait. Detection works best when one person or object dominates the frame.

How to do it in the app

1

Save the picture to Photos

If it lives in Files or another app, save an image to Recents first.

2

3

Export

Save a new image. Keep the original if you need an unblurred copy later.

Frequently asked questions

Can I blur a screenshot on iPhone?

Yes, if it is in Photos. Treat it like any other picture: open in How to Blur Photo and apply background or full-frame blur.

What if there is no person in the picture?

Detection still needs a dominant object. For flat scenes, a gentle full-image gaussian is usually cleaner than a fake subject mask.
