How to blur a photo on iPhone (from the camera roll)

Last updated: September 2026

How to blur a photo on iPhone is a camera-roll problem. The file exists. You either want a portrait-style background or a fully soft frame. Photos’ own tools do not offer a simple background blur for every older shot.

Blurring a photo on iPhone means editing an existing image so part or all of it is out of focus. How to Blur Photo handles subject-aware background blur and style filters (motion, gaussian, creative) on-device.

Two different results

Background blur: subject readable, scene soft. Full-frame blur: the entire photo looks hazy (privacy, stylized posts, or hiding detail). Decide which you want before you pick a style. Mixing them by accident is how edges look dirty.

This page is for the photo-on-iPhone query. If you said “picture,” see the sister guide—it covers screenshots and saved images with the same app.

How to do it in the app

1
