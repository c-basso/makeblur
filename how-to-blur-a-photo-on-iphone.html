How to blur a photo on iPhone — the whole image or just the background

Last updated: September 2026

Short answer

To blur a photo on iPhone, open it in How to Blur Photo and choose what to blur: the Full Photo tab softens the entire image, the Background tab keeps the subject sharp and blurs what is behind it. Pick a style such as Gaussian, drag Radius to set the strength, and tap Save. Photos on iPhone has no blur tool of its own for regular photos.

Download on the App Store

“How to blur a photo on iPhone” covers two different wishes. Some people want the entire picture soft — for a wallpaper, a story background, or to hide detail. Others want a portrait look where only the background is blurred. The built-in Photos app does neither on a normal photo, so this guide shows both in one app and explains when to use which.

What Photos can and cannot do

Photos → Edit offers exposure, colour, crop, and filters, but no blur slider for a regular photo. The only blur control is the f-number on Portrait-mode captures, and it only affects the background of those specific shots. Markup can cover parts of a picture with shapes, which hides rather than blurs. For any real blur — full image or background — you need a dedicated editor.

Which tab to use

Full Photo: the blur is applied evenly to every pixel. Use it for lock-screen wallpapers, text backgrounds, or when you want to share the mood of a picture without the detail. Background: the app detects the subject and blurs only what is behind it. Use it for portraits, product shots, and anything where a person or object should stand out.

Both tabs share the same style chips — Gaussian, Motion, Ghosting, Box, Pixelate, Hexagonal, Crystallize — and the same Radius slider. Motion adds an Angle slider for direction.

Choosing a style

Gaussian is the neutral, lens-like option and the right default. Ghosting produces a dreamy double image that suits full-photo edits for stories. Motion suggests speed. Pixelate, Box, Hexagonal, and Crystallize are stylised and are better for creative posts or privacy than for realism.

How to blur a photo in How to Blur Photo

  1. 1

    Open the photo

    Launch How to Blur Photo and select a picture from your library. Screenshots and downloaded images work too.

  2. 2

    Choose Full Photo or Background

    Tap Full Photo to blur everything, or leave Background selected to keep the subject sharp.

  3. 3

    Pick a style and drag Radius

    Gaussian is a safe start. Move Radius until the preview looks right; for a wallpaper go high, for a portrait stay moderate.

  4. 4

    Save

    Tap Save to export the blurred photo to your library.

Tips

  • For a phone wallpaper, use Full Photo with Gaussian at Radius 70–90 so icons stay readable on top.
  • For a portrait, use Background and stay below Radius 60.
  • If the app detects the wrong subject, the Manual tab lets you brush the blur exactly where you want it.

Frequently asked

Can I blur a photo in the iPhone Photos app?

Not a regular photo. Photos only adjusts the background blur of Portrait-mode captures via the f-number control. Use How to Blur Photo for any other image.

How do I blur only part of a photo?

Use the Manual tab and brush over the area. See the guide on blurring part of a picture for details.

Is the app free?

Yes, it is free to download with basic blur included; advanced styles are optional in-app purchases.
