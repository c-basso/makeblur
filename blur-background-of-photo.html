Blur background of a photo on iPhone

Last updated: September 2026

Blur background of a photo is a result, not a camera setting. You already have the image. You want the person or product to stand out and the clutter behind it to fall away—for a story, a listing, or a message.

To blur background of a photo is to soften out-of-focus areas while leaving the main subject readable. How to Blur Photo does that on iPhone after capture, with subject detection and intensity control, without uploading the file to a server to run the blur.

When this is the right tool

Use background blur when the subject should stay identifiable: a face, a pet, a plate of food, a product. If you need a transparent cutout for a shop template, that is background removal—a different product category.

Social posts and portraits are the listing’s stated use cases. The app is built around one job: blur photo backgrounds with the subject held.

Control, not a one-tap mystery

The store screenshots show intensity and region control, not only a filter preset. Start milder than you think; it is easier to add blur than to recover a melted edge. Check hair, glasses, and hands against the background before you save.

How to do it in the app

1

Choose the photo

Open How to Blur Photo and select the image whose background you want soft.

2

Confirm the subject

Detection should cover the person or object. Refine if a lamp or passer-by was included with the subject.

3

Blur, review edges, save

Apply background blur, zoom the edges, then save on-device.
