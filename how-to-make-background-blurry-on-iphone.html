How to make background blurry on iPhone

Last updated: September 2026

How to make background blurry on iPhone is the beginner wording for shallow depth of field. You do not need the word bokeh. You want the wall, street, or kitchen behind someone to go quiet.

Making a background blurry on iPhone means reducing detail behind a sharp subject. How to Blur Photo does this after you shoot, so you are not limited to Portrait mode at capture time.

If Portrait is greyed out

Portrait needs a compatible camera and enough light and distance. Night shots, wide groups, and photos sent by friends often cannot be converted inside Photos. The after-you-shoot app is for those files.

Start with a moderate blur. A background that is slightly soft still looks like a photo; an overcooked smear looks like a filter.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my iPhone background not blurry in Photos?

The shot likely has no Portrait depth. Use How to Blur Photo on the existing image instead of trying to enable Portrait after the fact.

Will this work on a photo someone sent me?

If it is in Recents, yes. There is no depth file attached to most shared JPEGs.
