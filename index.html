How to blur the background of a photo on iPhone

Three steps after the photo is already on your camera roll.

No Portrait-mode recapture. Open the shot, confirm the subject, save a blurred background.

1

Download How to Blur Photo

Install How to Blur Photo from the App Store (Make Blur). Requires iPhone with iOS 17.1 or later. Free to download; advanced styles are optional purchases.

2

Select a photo you already took

Open any image from Recents. The app detects the subject so the person or object stays sharp while you blur the background of the photo.

3

Adjust blur and save on-device

Set intensity, pick motion, gaussian, face blur, or a creative style, then save. Processing stays on your iPhone — photos are not uploaded to blur backgrounds.
