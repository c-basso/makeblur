How to blur the background of a photo on iPhone

No reshoot, no desktop editor. Open the photo you already have, confirm the subject, save.

    Download How to Blur Photo

    Free on the App Store for iPhone with iOS 18.6 or later. Open it and pick any photo from your library — a normal Photo-mode shot, a selfie, a screenshot, or an image someone sent you.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions people search before they download. Each one links to a full guide.

How do I blur the background on iPhone after taking the photo?

Open the photo in How to Blur Photo, keep the Background tab selected so the subject is detected automatically, choose Gaussian, drag Radius until the background is as soft as you want, and tap Save. It works on any photo in your library — Portrait mode is not required.

Read the guide →

Can the iPhone Photos app blur the background of an existing photo?

Only if the photo was captured in Portrait mode, using the f-number control in Edit. Normal photos, selfies without Portrait, screenshots, and received images have no depth data, so Photos offers no background blur for them. That is what this app is for.

Read the guide →

How do I blur faces in a photo on iPhone without Apple Intelligence?

Tap the Faces tab in How to Blur Photo. Every face in the frame is detected and blurred at once; choose Pixelate or Gaussian and set Radius. It runs on any iPhone with iOS 18.6 or later — no Pro model or Apple Intelligence needed.

Read the guide →

How do I blur just part of a picture on iPhone?

Use the Manual tab: brush over the area — a name, a house number, a person in the background — pick a style, set Radius, and Save. Only the painted region is blurred. Photos’ Markup can only cover areas with shapes.

Read the guide →

How do I blur text or a license plate in a photo?

Open the image, tap Manual, choose Pixelate or Box, brush over the text or plate, and raise Radius until no character is readable when you zoom in. Then Save and share the exported copy, not the original.

Read the guide →

How do I add a motion blur effect to a photo?

Tap the Motion style on the Background tab. Radius sets how long the streaks are and Angle sets their direction; match the angle to the way the subject is moving. The subject stays sharp while the background streaks.

Read the guide →

What is the difference between gaussian blur and bokeh?

Gaussian is a smooth, even blur — the closest software match to a lens out of focus. Bokeh describes how out-of-focus highlights render. On the Background tab, Gaussian or Ghosting at a higher Radius gives the bokeh look on backgrounds with lights.

How do I make a whole photo blurry on iPhone?

Tap Full Photo instead of Background, choose Gaussian or Ghosting, drag Radius, and Save. It is the quickest way to make a soft wallpaper or a story background from any picture.

Is How to Blur Photo free?

Yes, it is free to download and basic background blur is included. Advanced styles and tools are optional in-app purchases — $4.99 and $29.99 on the US App Store as of September 2026.

Are my photos uploaded when I blur them?

No. The App Store listing states photos are processed on your device and are not uploaded to external servers. The app works without an internet connection.

Any iPhone running iOS 18.6 or later. It is iPhone-only (no iPad version) and available in English and 30 other languages.

How do I pixelate a face or part of a photo?

Choose Faces (for people) or Manual (for an area), tap the Pixelate style, and drag Radius — it controls block size. Use blocks larger than an eye or a letter for real privacy.
