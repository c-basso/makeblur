Blur photo background on iPhone

Last updated: September 2026

Blur photo background is how people type when they want an app, not a photography class. The App Store listing repeats photo blur background and blur photo background for that reason. This page is the website match: what the app does, what it costs, what it will not do.

Photo background blur is a subject-sharp, background-soft edit on a still image. How to Blur Photo is a free-to-download iPhone app (Graphics & Design) that performs that edit on-device after you shoot.

What you get vs what you pay

US listing (September 2026): free download, basic background blur included, advanced styles and tools via in-app purchases at $4.99 and $29.99, plus a subscription SKU with a trial for “Make Blur Background Photo Effect.” Check the live store for your country.

It will not remove a background to transparent PNG. It will not blur video. It is iPhone-first; the listing says only for iPhone.
