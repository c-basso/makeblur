How to blur faces in a photo on iPhone

Last updated: September 2026

How to blur faces in a photo is a privacy task: kids in the background, a stranger in a street frame, coworkers who did not agree to a story. You need identities unreadable without destroying the rest of the picture.

Face blur hides facial features so a person is not easily identified in a still image. How to Blur Photo includes a face-blur flow (see the App Store screenshot). It runs on iOS 17.1+ and does not require Apple Intelligence–only iPhones.

Apple Photos Clean Up vs this app

iOS 18 Clean Up can pixelate faces, but only on Apple Intelligence hardware (iPhone 15 Pro and iPhone 16 class, plus the software that supports it). Many phones that can run iOS 17.1 cannot use that filter. Markup rectangles look amateur on a feed.

If your iPhone is not in the Intelligence set, or Clean Up keeps removing the whole head, a dedicated face-blur tool is the reliable path. Blur, then share; do not rely on a platform’s “only me” setting.

Do it before you send

Once an unblurred original leaves your phone, you cannot pull it back. Apply face blur, save a copy, and send the copy. Keep the original in a private album if you still need it.

Export, then share that file. Confirm identities are unreadable at story size, not only on a 6.7-inch screen.

Frequently asked questions

Does face blur need Apple Intelligence?

No. How to Blur Photo lists iOS 17.1 as the floor, not an Intelligence chip. That is the conversion reason for this guide.

Is face blur the same as background blur?

No. Background blur keeps faces of the subject; face blur hides identities. You can use both on one photo.
